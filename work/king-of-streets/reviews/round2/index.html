What this round is

Thirteen items: the two riders and the helmet (B1 to B3), and each rider on each of the five approved bikes (C1 to C10, the man first). The bikes are the frozen round 1 files and were not changed. Every picture is drawn from the compressed file the game would load, in the game's own 3D settings, and every number below was measured on that compressed file put back on the bike, not on a working copy.

What I need you to decide

  1. B1, the man's clothes. The only realistic rigged adult man I could find free is in a waistcoat and tie. A man in a T-shirt and jeans exists as a free model but has no skeleton. A rigged one in a T-shirt is a stylised boy.
  2. B2, the woman's top. Its upper edge looks torn close up because her hair was painted onto it in the source. Two other women I tried wear stiletto heels. If you will not accept this one, tell me, and I will say plainly whether a free one exists.
  3. C1 and C6, the scooter. The pose is correct to the grips and feet, but hunched. The scooter's grips are as low as its seat.
  4. Helmet colours are placeholders (B3).

What I could not check

How it looks on your iPhone: I draw in software, at the phone's size. Whether the lean looks right is for you to say. At race distance the riders are small, as in the phone pictures.

How each number was measured

  • Hands. The grip on each bike was measured by firing rays at it (radius and axis to about 2 mm). Then how far each hand vertex sits inside it, and whether rays through the front of the wrap reach bare grip.
  • Feet. The lowest points of each foot against the peg or floor below them.
  • Seat and body. Rider points against the bike's volume, in three directions.
