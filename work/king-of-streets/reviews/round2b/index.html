What changed after your feedback

  • Seated. I was measuring one point touching the seat, which passed things that clearly were not seated. Now I measure how much of her underside lies on it, and the fit presses the pelvis and thighs onto the seat, as weight does. In the zone where she sits, 6 to 18% of the woman touched the seat before; now 44 to 64%. A cut-away through the middle of the bike shows it in every item.
  • The man was distorted. That was my mistake: I stretched the small pictures. They are now drawn at their true shape. He was also low quality because no free model is good: see B1.
  • A man in riding gear. B1 is now the woman’s leather suit, reshaped for a man and coloured blue, so he has the same quality and the same gear. The man on each bike is C1 to C5.

What I need you to decide

  1. B1, the man. Is the reshaped suit good enough? From behind it reads as a man; from the front it still looks like a woman’s suit. If not, no free model is better, so tell me whether to repair the low-detail biker, or something else.
  2. Helmet colours are placeholders (B3).

What is not perfect

Her thighs rise off the seat towards the knees, as real thighs do on these bikes, so the seat share is never 100%. On the scooter the rider presses up to 28 mm into the soft cushion, and on the others a shin, knee or fingertip passes 4 to 24 mm into the bodywork. A few fingertip pads sit up to 4.4 mm into a grip. The shoes are low, not tall boots. Every picture is drawn from the compressed file the game would load, and every number was measured on it, put back on the frozen bike. The bikes were not changed.
