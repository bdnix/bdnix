King of Streets riders, second try

Round 2 again, after you rejected everything. Eight items: the man as a decision, the woman, the helmet, and the woman on each bike. Nothing here is approved yet.

What changed after your feedback

  • Riding gear. The woman is a new model in a one-piece leather suit and gloves. Nothing was cut from her back.
  • Helmet. A new, detailed racing helmet with a dark visor, makers’ logos removed.
  • Seated. The fitting now lowers the rider until her buttocks rest on the seat, and every C item is measured for it.
  • Scooter. She sits upright on it, knees together, feet flat on the floorboard.

What I need you to decide

  1. B1, the man. There is no free, realistic, rigged man in riding gear. Two options are shown, or the woman’s leathers on both. C1 to C5 wait for this.
  2. Helmet colours are placeholders (B3).

What is not perfect

On the scooter her crotch presses up to 29 mm into the front of the soft seat cushion, and on A2, A4 and A5 a shin dips 12 to 17 mm into the bodywork beside it. A few fingertip pads sit up to 3.8 mm into a grip. Her shoes are low black shoes, not tall boots. Every picture is drawn from the compressed file the game would load, and every number was measured on it, put back on the frozen bike. The bikes were not changed.

decision

Option 1: bike rider 3d

A one-piece car-racing suit, gloves and boots, rigged. A game avatar, less realistic than B2. The suit carries car sponsors' logos, which would be painted out. Its helmet would be replaced by B3.

Option 2: Just man

A realistic man in a brown leather jacket, trousers and trainers, rigged. Not riding gear: no gloves, and the jacket is a casual one. His face and hair would be removed under B3, and his 44,760 triangles cut to fit the 20,000 budget.
