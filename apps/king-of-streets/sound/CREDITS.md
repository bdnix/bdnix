# King of Streets sounds

The engines and the bike passing by are real recordings, all in the public domain, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). They have been cut, looped and levelled.

- `engine-low.wav`: from [Jawa250 motorbike exhaust sound.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jawa250_motorbike_exhaust_sound.ogg), by Viktor Khodyeyev, public domain.
- `engine-high.wav`: from [Jawa350 motorbike exhaust sound.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jawa350_motorbike_exhaust_sound.ogg), by Viktor Khodyeyev, public domain.
- `pass.wav`: from [Moped driving by.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moped_driving_by.ogg), by stephan (PDSounds), public domain.

Everything else the game plays (the street's hum, CNGs, diesels, horns, the wind, the tyres and the blows) is made as it plays, with the Web Audio API.
