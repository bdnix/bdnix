# King of Streets textures and skies

The photo textures and skies are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no rights reserved. They have been shrunk and saved as WebP (textures) and smaller Radiance HDR files (skies).

- Textures: Asphalt 02, Concrete Pavement, Plastered Wall 04, Worn Plaster Wall, White Plaster 02, Concrete Wall 006 and Concrete Tiles.
- Skies (`sky/`): Qwantani Night, Kloofendal Misty Morning, Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy and Qwantani Dusk 2, all "pure sky".
