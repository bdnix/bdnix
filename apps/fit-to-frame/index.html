Resize Without Cropping

Make a photo fit a post, a story or a banner without cropping any of it. Space is added around the image, in the colour you choose, until it’s the right shape. Everything happens in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.

  • Social media shapes. Square, portrait 4:5, story 9:16, landscape, widescreen and more, or type your own width and height.
  • Nothing cropped. The whole image stays in view, in the middle, with space added to its sides or to its top and bottom.
  • Any colour. White, black, grey, or pick any colour for the space around the image, and add a margin all round if you like.
  • Full quality or an exact size. Keep every pixel of the original, or get exactly the size in pixels a site asks for.
  • Private. Location, camera and other hidden details are removed from the saved images.
