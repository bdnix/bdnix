Photo Collage

Put 3, 6 or 9 photos together in one picture, to share or print. Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded.

  • 7 to 10 layouts for 3, 6 or 9 photos. Grids, one or two big photos with smaller ones around them, steps, columns and mosaics.
  • Shapes for where it’s going. Square, portrait 4:5 for feeds, 9:16 for stories, landscape 3:2 or widescreen 16:9.
  • Your style. Choose the spacing between photos, round the corners and pick the background colour.
  • Rearrange. Tap two photos, in the preview or the list, to swap them. Drag a photo in the preview to choose which part of it shows in its space, and zoom it in up to 4× with a pinch, the mouse wheel or a slider.
  • Sharp and private. Download a JPEG or PNG up to 4096 px. Location and camera details aren’t carried over.
