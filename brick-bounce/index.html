Brick Bounce

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Don’t let it past you. Glowing bricks drop powers: catch them for more balls, a fireball, a laser, a wider paddle or an extra ball.

← → or the mouse to move · Space or click to launch · P pause
