Unlock a PDF

Open a password-protected PDF with its password, and download a copy that opens without one. Everything happens in your browser. Your file and your password are never uploaded.

  • You need the password: this removes a password you know, it doesn’t guess one.
  • Works with the user password (the one that opens the file) or the owner password, which also lifts limits on printing, copying and editing.
  • The pages, text and links stay exactly as they were. Only the encryption is taken off.
